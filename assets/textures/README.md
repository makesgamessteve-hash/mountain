# Textures

All textures in this directory are procedurally generated by
`tools/gen_assets.py` (deterministic: re-running it produces byte-identical
files). They are licensed **CC BY 4.0**, © Mountain contributors.

Regenerate with `python3 tools/gen_assets.py` from the repository root. Add
`--preview DIR` to also write upscaled contact sheets.

General conventions:

- All files are 8-bit RGBA PNGs.
- In atlases, tile index `i` is at column `i % 8`, row `i / 8`. Row 0 is at the
  **top** of the image.
- Alpha-cutout sprites use hard alpha (0 or 255). Their transparent pixels carry
  the average colour of the sprite's opaque edge, so filtering doesn't produce
  dark fringes.
- Lighting baked into sprites and materials comes from the upper left.
- Unlisted tiles are fully transparent.

## foliage.png (512x512, 8x8 tiles of 64x64)

**GRAY** tiles are neutral grayscale, with luminance roughly 0.45–1.0 and a
common median of about 0.68. The shader multiplies them by a per-species,
per-season tint. **COLOR** tiles are full colour and are not tinted. Plants
(grass, flowers, ferns, mushrooms, reeds) stand on the bottom edge of the tile.
Clusters are centred blobs.

## terrain.png (256x64)

Four 64x64 seamlessly tileable grayscale detail textures, left to right:

| Index | x range | Content |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 0 | 0–63    | Grass / meadow ground (clumps, blade strokes, soil gaps) |
| 1 | 64–127  | Rock (warped strata, cracks) |
| 2 | 128–191 | Dirt / gravel path (shaded pebbles on soil) |
| 3 | 192–255 | Snow (subtle drifts and wind ripples) |

Each tile's mean value is 128 (0.5). The shader computes
`base_color * tex * 2`. Alpha is 255 everywhere.

## moon.png (128x128)

A realistic full moon in grayscale: maria laid out like the lunar near side,
craters, and Tycho/Copernicus ray systems. The disc has radius 62 px and is
centred at (64, 64). Alpha is 0 outside the disc, with a one-pixel
anti-aliased rim.
